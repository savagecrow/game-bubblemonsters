Bubble Monsters

Play Online

Co-op against the monsters. One of you hosts and shares the 4-letter code, the other joins with it. You play the green monster, your friend plays the blue one.

How to Play

Move AD / arrows   Jump W / Space / ↑ (hold for a higher jump; press again in mid-air for a double jump, once more for a triple)   Blow bubble J / Z / F   Bubble shield hold K / X / S

Trap: blow a bubble at a monster to lock it inside. Pop the bubble (touch it from the side or below) to burst the monster into fruit. Leave it too long and it breaks out angry.

Chain: bursting bubbles that touch each other pops the whole cluster, and every pop in the chain doubles the points.

Ride: bubbles float up and are solid from above, so land on one and ride it. Hold jump to hop off it with a boost.

Bounce: glowing fruit (melon, pumpkin, plum) keep bouncing: land on top of one to spring much higher. They pop after three bounces for a bonus. Normal fruit is just points.

Teleporters: the holes in the floor and ceiling are bubble teleporters. Fall into one and you come out of the other. Some stages also have mid-air teleporter bubble pairs.

Shield: hold the shield key to wrap yourself in a bubble: enemies bounce off, but it drains and then needs to recharge, and you can't shoot while it's up.

Drop through: platforms are one-way. To come back down, hold the shield / down key and press jump while standing on one.

Power orbs (dropped by some monsters, timed): S speed, W wide long-range bubbles, R rapid fire, O full shield + brief invulnerability.

Bosses arrive every 5th stage. They can't be trapped: hit them with bubbles until their bar is empty. They summon minions and get furious when low. P pauses, M mutes the music.

Hurry: dawdle and the monsters turn angry; keep dawdling and an unstoppable ghost comes for you. Clear all monsters to move on. Online, you play together: the host is green, the guest is blue. Two on one keyboard: green ADW F shoot G shield; blue arrows, . shoot / shield.
